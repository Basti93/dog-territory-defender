## 2. Works end-to-end vs fake / hardcoded / stubbed (pre-filled)
Works for real: GPS tracking with foreground service, Home Range grid + Bloom, treats/XP payout, Den upgrades, stats and achievements, local SQLite persistence, Backup/Restore, de/en.
- No backend, no accounts, no social layer: everything is local to one phone.
- Reminders are inexact (no `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) and re-arm only when the app is opened or a walk is saved; they stop after 7 unopened days.
- Sounds and the music box are synthetic (generated by script), not recorded.
- Buddy appears in medium size only as art (size is a uniform scale); markings are overlays on the head layer; accent colour is baked in.
- Territory names come from reverse-geocoding and stay in the language they were created in.
- Den art has 3 growth stages for building levels up to 100 (thresholds picked by eye).
- Map tiles need internet; the Doorstep portrait falls back to a neutral line if capture fails.
- Phase 2+ features (streaks, leaderboards, Pack) are intentionally absent.
