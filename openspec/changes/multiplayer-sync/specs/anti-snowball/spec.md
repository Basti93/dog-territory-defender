## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Neighbour sprinkle sits outside the power budget
The treat sprinkle from a simulated neighbour dog's gift SHALL be income proportional to the household's own recent walking (5 percent of its average treats per walk), SHALL NOT be a capped percentage bonus, and therefore SHALL NOT count toward the +30% power budget. It SHALL NOT be multiplied by Beginner's Luck or by any building, Pack or badge bonus, SHALL be limited to one per day per household, and SHALL NOT depend on Buddy's Level beyond the proportionality of the household's own average.

#### Scenario: Veteran and newcomer on equal walks
- **WHEN** a level-100 and a level-1 household open a sprinkle after equal recent walks
- **THEN** each SHALL receive 5 percent of its own average walk, so the ratio between them SHALL equal the ratio of their walk income and SHALL NOT be widened by the sprinkle

#### Scenario: Sprinkle inside Beginner's Luck
- **WHEN** a household inside its Beginner's Luck window opens a sprinkle
- **THEN** the doubling SHALL NOT apply to it
