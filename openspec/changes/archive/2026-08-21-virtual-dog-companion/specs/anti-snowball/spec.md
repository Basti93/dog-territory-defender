## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Hard Power Gap Soft-Cap
The combined advantage from all *power* sources (Den building bonuses, Pack, badges) that a level-100 player can hold over a level-1 player on equivalent walks SHALL be capped at a maximum of +30% — high enough that veterans don't feel their grind was meaningless, low enough that new players don't give up. Buddy's Level (Den Level) SHALL NOT count toward this budget: it drives a per-walk *income* rate (treats earned), not a capped bonus percentage applied to a defense/XP/find-rate quantity, so it contributes 0% regardless of how high it climbs. No future capped percentage bonus SHALL ever be defined as a function of Buddy's Level or of a player's treat balance — only of building level, Pack, or badges.

#### Scenario: Combined power gap ceiling
- **WHEN** all applicable capped-power bonuses (Den building, Pack, badge percentages) are summed for a level-100 player versus a level-1 player on an equivalent walk
- **THEN** the combined advantage SHALL NOT exceed +30%

#### Scenario: Level contributes no power
- **WHEN** Buddy's Level is evaluated as a candidate power source for the combined power gap ceiling above
- **THEN** its contribution SHALL be 0%, regardless of how high Buddy's Level climbs
