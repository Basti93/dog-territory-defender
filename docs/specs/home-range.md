# Home Range Specification

A persistent, per-player spatial grid replacing the one-polygon-per-walk territory model, with two rendering layers (the Range: permanent monotonic outline of everywhere ever walked; the Bloom: colour-only freshness fill) plus a `visits` ghost-trail brightness channel. Numeric thresholds are load-bearing; read `docs/decisions/2026-08-21-home-range.md` before changing any.

- **Grid, not shapes:** territory is a persistent grid of fixed 20m square cells, each with its own `lastRefreshed`, `lastTracked` and `visits`. A cell walked again by a later walk takes the more recent walk's state (no second record).
- **Monotonic Range:** the Range (every cell ever painted by a live-GPS-tracked walk) only grows. No action, including deleting a saved walk from history, removes a cell; the total never decreases from one walk to the next and the Range never visibly shrinks.
- **Every tracked walk paints, any shape:** all cells within the painting radius of a live-tracked walk's path are painted, loop or not (an out-and-back street paints its full path), subject to the anti-exploit segment guards: cells between two fixes are not painted if they are farther apart than the maximum segment gap or imply travel faster than the maximum walking speed (no false corridor through a dropout or non-walking travel).
- **Bloom is colour only:** freshness derives only from time since last refresh and shows as hue/saturation, never a number, percentage, count or bar. Every Range cell keeps constant fill opacity, so an old cell differs only in colour and never fades toward the base map.
- **Hard ceiling:** a cell's wildness reaches its maximum within 18 days of last refresh and never changes after that (18 days = 18 months).
- **New Ground ignores jitter:** "New Ground" (cells painted by this walk that are neither in the Range nor adjacent to a Range cell) uses an 8-neighbour dilation against the Range. A known route walked again with normal GPS jitter does not fire it; a street with no painted cell within one cell fires it once enough new cells clear the detection threshold.
- **One-time seeding:** every previously saved walk with a real GPS trail is replayed into the Home Range exactly once (not again on later app opens), using the walk's own recorded time to seed freshness, so historical walks show their true age (a year-old walk paints fully wild cells, not fresh ones). Manually logged walks (no trail) are not replayed, since they have no GPS evidence of ground.
