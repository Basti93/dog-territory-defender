# Home Range Specification

A persistent, per-player spatial grid replacing the one-polygon-per-walk territory model, with two rendering layers (the Range: permanent monotonic outline of everywhere ever walked; the Bloom: colour-only freshness fill) plus a `visits` ghost-trail brightness channel. Numeric thresholds are load-bearing; read `docs/decisions/2026-08-21-home-range.md` before changing any.

- **Grid, not shapes:** territory is a persistent grid of fixed 20m square cells, each with its own `lastRefreshed`, `lastTracked` and `visits`. A cell walked again by a later walk takes the more recent walk's state (no second record).
- **Monotonic Range:** the Range (every cell ever painted by a live-GPS-tracked walk) only grows. No action, including deleting a saved walk from history, removes a cell; the total never decreases from one walk to the next and the Range never visibly shrinks.
- **Every tracked walk paints, any shape:** all cells within the painting radius of a live-tracked walk's path are painted, loop or not (an out-and-back street paints its full path), subject to the anti-exploit segment guards: cells between two fixes are not painted if they are farther apart than the maximum segment gap or imply travel faster than the maximum walking speed (no false corridor through a dropout or non-walking travel).
- **Bloom is colour only:** freshness derives only from time since last refresh and shows as hue/saturation, never a number, percentage, count or bar. Every Range cell keeps constant fill opacity, so an old cell differs only in colour and never fades toward the base map.
- **Hard ceiling:** a cell's wildness reaches its maximum within 18 days of last refresh and never changes after that (18 days = 18 months).
- **New Ground ignores jitter:** "New Ground" (cells painted by this walk that are neither in the Range nor adjacent to a Range cell) uses an 8-neighbour dilation against the Range. A known route walked again with normal GPS jitter does not fire it; a street with no painted cell within one cell fires it once enough new cells clear the detection threshold.
- **One-time seeding:** every previously saved walk with a real GPS trail is replayed into the Home Range exactly once (not again on later app opens), using the walk's own recorded time to seed freshness, so historical walks show their true age (a year-old walk paints fully wild cells, not fresh ones). Manually logged walks (no trail) are not replayed, since they have no GPS evidence of ground.

## Unsere Ecken (named favourite corners)

Places the household keeps coming back to become corners: a small wood sign on the Viertel map with a name. Cosmetic: no treats, no rank, no counter beyond the stats sign ("N Ecken", household-wide, never compared). Numbers live in `src/economy/corners.ts` (`CORNER_CONFIG`).
- **Detection (pure, on every phone, `src/utils/cornerDetection.ts`):** a 3x3-cell block (60 m) that tracked walks passed through on at least 4 different local days. Days count per walk, not per paint (one walk passing twice, or two walks on one day, count once; manual logs have no route and do not count). New corners lie at least 200 m apart; none within 150 m of home (home = first walk's start, the median start of the first 30 once there are 5); at most 1 new corner per walk (the walk that completes the fourth day) and at most 1 per 7 local days (judged by walk dates in the replay, not the wall clock; a qualified block simply waits for the first walk after the week). Why: favourite places, not every street; one naming moment per week at most. Detection replays the walks oldest first, so two phones holding the same walks get the same corners. Corners already stored are never deleted when these numbers change (only new ones are gated); the Viertel folds two corners into one only when they are closer than 150 m (`displayDedupeM`).
- **Identity:** the block's centre cell id `"ix,iy"` (tie: most days, then the middle of the qualifying stretch). Two phones never disagree about an id; if sync lag still gives one street two ids, display keeps one (named first, then older, then lower id).
- **Name:** never blank. The household's name (1-24 characters, either member, last writer wins, no notification), else "Ecke {street}" / "{street} corner" from the OS geocoder (`resolveStreetForPoint`), else "Unsere Ecke" / "Our corner". The tap sheet says "Name von {member}" only when the other member wrote the name.
- **Discovery:** the walk that completes a corner gets one homecoming beat-3 extra (first in line, counts as one of the max 2 extras together with badges): Buddy sits, "Hier seid ihr oft. Gebt der Ecke einen Namen?" with one button; any other tap skips it for free and it never repeats.
- **Display:** signs on the embedded Viertel and the full map; tapping a sign on the full map opens a PaperNote sheet (rename, Buddy lying, the newest 3 postcards of walks that ran through the block). A postcard's postmark uses the corner's name when the walk touched a named corner (else the territory).
- **Sync:** names are household data (`corner.name` command, `corner.updated` event, snapshot `corners`); detection is not synced. After a restore from backup the corners are re-found from the walks, names are lost.
