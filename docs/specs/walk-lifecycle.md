# walk-lifecycle Specification

How a walk starts and ends (finished: trail preserved and painted into the Home Range, any shape, as long as it went anywhere; or discarded via Cancel), the no-movement rule, and the ground-covered floor that gates rewards.

## Painting
- Every live-GPS-tracked walk with movement paints the Home Range grid (see `den-economy`'s Home Range) from its recorded trail, regardless of whether start and end are near each other (a far-apart walk paints exactly like a loop).

## Finish and Cancel
- Exactly two terminal actions while a walk runs: **Finish Walk** (stops tracking, saves the trail, paints its cells regardless of shape, evaluates reward eligibility; no-movement walks excepted, see below) and **Cancel** (discards the walk: no trail, no Home Range effect).
- Cancel is reachable from a calm "Runde abbrechen" link under the Finish button on the live map and a small "abbrechen" link under the scene's gate sign.
- Cancelling a walk that has moved asks once ("Runde verwerfen? Diese Runde wird nicht gespeichert." with "Verwerfen" and "Weiterlaufen"); "Weiterlaufen" leaves the walk running untouched. A walk without movement is cancelled without the question.
- Cancel stops GPS and the foreground service, clears the stored active walk, closes the live presence (`ended: true`), and leaves rewards, stats and walk count untouched (no trail shown). Afterwards the player is on the scene and Buddy may say one friendly line ("Dann eben später!"); no wording blames the player.
- While a walk runs, the gate sign reads "Zur Runde" and opens the live map (tapping sign or gate starts no second walk). Only the gate and its sign start a walk; tapping Buddy never does (it only barks).

## A walk that went nowhere leaves no record
- A tracked walk has no movement when (a) every fix lies within 40 m of the first fix (largest haversine distance from the start under 40 m, however far the fixes sum up: GPS jitter around a standing phone; e.g. 20 minutes of +-15 m noise summing to hundreds of metres is still no walk), or (b) its walked distance (`walkDistanceMeters`) is under 50 m AND it paints fewer than 13 Home Range cells.
- Rule: `walkHasMovement` in `src/utils/geometry.ts`; constants `NO_MOVEMENT_RADIUS_M`, `NO_MOVEMENT_MAX_DISTANCE_M`, `NO_MOVEMENT_CELLS_BELOW` in `src/utils/grid.ts`.
- Such a walk is not saved: no walk record, route, Home Range cells, stats, reward, `walk.upload` or homecoming.
- Finish Walk: closed like a cancel, player lands on the scene, Buddy says "Noch keine Strecke, nichts zu speichern." Auto-finish (still for 20 minutes with fixes arriving, or back home) and cold-start restore: discarded quietly.
- Tracked walks only; a manually logged walk has no fixes by design and is never judged by it (saved and rewarded as before).
- Why: a mistaken start or standing phone used to leave an empty record that counted as a walk. The cell half is calibrated to the paint footprint: a walk that never leaves one spot paints about 5 cells (25 m paint radius on 20 m cells), 50 m of walking about 12, so a floor of 2 could never hold; 13 means "less ground than 50 m of walking paints" and only backs up the distance test.

## Auto-finish never judges missing data
- Auto-finish ("Runde automatisch beenden", on by default) ends a walk (a) "home": back within 40 m of the start after having been 150 m away, for 3 minutes, walk at least 8 minutes old, or (b) "still": accepted fixes kept arriving but stayed within 25 m of the last moving position for 20 minutes. Rules: `src/app/autoFinish.ts`.
- **Only evidence counts.** If no accepted fix arrived for 5 minutes (`GPS_GAP_MS`) that is a GPS gap (battery saver, frozen or killed app, phone in a pocket), not standing still: no auto-finish decision is made, the walk keeps running, `WALK_GPS_GAP {minutes}` is logged once. The first fix after a gap restarts the still and home clocks (what happened in the gap is unknown), so one fix after a 23-minute silence can never end the walk.
- A gap of 20 minutes or more (`GPS_GAP_NOTE_MS`) shows a calm line on the live map while the app is in front, for 20 seconds: "GPS hatte eine Pause (Energiesparmodus?) – die Runde läuft weiter." / "GPS took a break (battery saver?) – the walk is still on." Coming back to the front re-evaluates at once.
- **The stored fixes are the truth.** The location task stores every accepted fix in the active-walk store, also while the UI side is frozen or in another runtime. When memory has seen no fix for a gap (and on coming back to the front) the session loads the store, takes over the newer fixes and replays them through the same rule on their own timestamps (`WALK_FIXES_FROM_STORE {count}`): gap logs are true, and "still" works while backgrounded. A "still" ending (live or restore) cuts the walk at the moment the dog stopped moving (`trimStillTail`): the idle tail does not count for duration, distance or rewards. Other endings are not trimmed.
- Cost of the rule: a dog that really sleeps under the café table with no fix arriving at all keeps the walk open (fixes need a 2 m move to be delivered); the player ends it, or the cold-start restore finishes it once it is 3 hours old.
- **Cold-start restore** (`restoreWalk.ts`, `useWalkRestore.ts`): the stored walk is replayed through the same rule. Newest fix under 3 hours old: resumed, however long the silence (a gap is resumed, never discarded or ended as "still"); 3 hours or older: finished as it stands (or dropped if it never moved or has under 2 points); replay ending "home" or "still" on real fixes finishes it (dropped if it never moved). Every launch logs `WALK_RESTORE_CHECK {found, points, ageMin, decision, ms}` (decision: none, busy, already-saved, discard, finish, resume).
- **Start waits for the restore.** Starting a walk replaces the stored one, so "Gassi gehen" is held until the restore has decided (at most 10 s, note "Runde wird wiederhergestellt…" on the scene). If the restore resumed a walk, no second walk starts.

## GPS fix accuracy (trail 50 m, painting 30 m)
- Fixes up to 50 m accuracy are kept (`MAX_ACCEPTABLE_ACCURACY_M` in `src/utils/gps.ts`; was 30 m): they form the trail, count for distance, movement and auto-finish. Worse fixes are logged as `GPS_FIX_REJECTED` and dropped.
- Home Range cells and corner cells are painted only from fixes of 30 m or better (`PAINT_MAX_ACCURACY_M` in `src/utils/grid.ts`, inside `paintedCells`/`walkedCells`, so phone and server agree). A rough fix is skipped for painting; the path continues between the good fixes under the usual 60 m / 4.5 m/s segment guards. Fixes without accuracy count as good. Live paint cues follow the same split.
- Why: a phone in a trouser pocket reports 30-50 m most of the time; the 30 m limit threw away most of such a walk (2026-10-19 morning walk). Painting keeps the old precision so a 45 m fix never stamps cells up to 70 m off the path. Tracking quality, not a balance change: reward floors (25 cells, movement rule) are unchanged, but a pocket walk now has fewer paint-grade fixes than a hand-held one.

## Android: keep walks running in the pocket
- Android battery saver and vendor battery optimisation freeze or kill the app and switch GPS off with the screen locked; the foreground service cannot prevent that. The app cannot detect it (no native module), so it points at the setting: a one-time dialog 8 seconds after the first walk start (Android only, stored as shown) and a permanent "Runden in der Tasche" section in Settings. Button: the system battery-optimisation list (`android.settings.IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATION_SETTINGS`), falling back to the app's system page. Copy: battery saver often switches GPS off in the pocket; turn it off or exempt Tails Up; set battery to "Nicht eingeschränkt".
- **Start from the background.** Android refuses to start the foreground service while the app is not in front. The walk is not cancelled: the start stays pending (`GPS_WATCH_DEFERRED`), a calm notification asks to open the app for a moment ("Öffne die App kurz, dann zählt eure Runde weiter mit."), and the task starts when the app next comes to the front (`GPS_WATCH_RESUMED_IN_FRONT`). No guilt copy.
- **Start order.** Tapping start starts the location task first (while the app is in front), then takes the initial fix; fixes arriving before the walk record exists are buffered and replayed. On Android, a start while the app is not active skips the doomed service call and goes straight to the deferred start (the refusal handler stays as safety net). Resuming a walk whose task still runs re-attaches the callback; in front on Android the task is started again anyway (a headless-restarted task has no foreground service), from the background the restart waits for the front (`GPS_WATCH_REATTACHED`). A cancelled start never stops or restarts a task a newer start owns. Why: a phone locked within seconds of the tap lost the foreground service (prod logs 18.10., 20.10.).
- **Stale first fix (iOS).** A start position older than 30 s (or rougher than 50 m) is asked for again once; delivered fixes older than 30 s before the task started are dropped (`GPS_FIX_STALE`); if the walk's start point is still stale when the first real fix arrives, that fix replaces it (`WALK_START_FIX_REPLACED`), so no walk opens with a jump.
- Diagnostics in the server log: `APP_LAUNCH {cold, sinceLastLogSec}` once per JS start (also a headless start), `APP_STATE {from,to}`, `GPS_FG_SERVICE {started, registered, appState}`. The Android foreground service only starts while the app is in front (expo-location); a location task the OS re-creates headless after killing the process runs without it.

## Reward eligibility is ground covered
- Treats, XP and any territory-derived bonus for a live-tracked walk only when it paints at least 25 Home Range cells (~200m of real ground), whatever its shape or start/end proximity. This replaces the old loop-closure gate (>=10 points AND start-end distance under a closure radius).
- A walk with movement but fewer than 25 cells is still saved and paints its cells, but gets no reward (none granted or displayed).

## Walked distance counts only plausible steps
- A step between consecutive fixes counts only if (normal) at most 60 m AND at most 4.5 m/s (same limits as the Home Range paint filter), or (dropout bridge) more than 60 m and at most 250 m, within 180 s, at an average of at most 2.0 m/s. A step with missing, zero or negative time difference counts only if at most 60 m.
- Examples: a 3 km step in 60 s (tram) adds nothing; a fix 150 m away and 100 s later (1.5 m/s) counts and the trail stays connected.
- Constants in `src/utils/grid.ts`: `MAX_SEGMENT_M`, `MAX_SEGMENT_SPEED_MS`, `DROPOUT_BRIDGE_MAX_M`, `DROPOUT_BRIDGE_MAX_S`, `DROPOUT_BRIDGE_MAX_SPEED_MS`; rule `stepCountsAsDistance` in `src/utils/geometry.ts`.
- Live distance, finished distance (rewards, personal stats, Walk Portrait) and the server all use the one function `walkDistanceMeters`, so they never disagree.
- Silent: the app never shows that part of a walk was not counted. The trail line is cut wherever a step fails the rule, so no straight line crosses a jump, ride or long signal loss (`splitAtGaps`; tested web copy in `src/web/gapSplitScript.ts`).
- Why: GPS jumps, tram/car rides and the gap after an iOS force-quit used to inflate distance, XP and lifetime/weekly kilometres. The bridge keeps honest signal losses (tunnel, underpass, sleeping phone) from costing a fair walk its distance: such a gap is short and crossed at a stroll, a ride is fast or far.
- Not retroactive: a walk that started before 2026-10-18 19:35 UTC (`DISTANCE_RULE_SINCE_MS`) keeps the old unfiltered distance (`cumulativeDistanceLegacy`) wherever distance is recomputed from points (Walk Portrait, prior-walk XP average, server weekly kilometres). Stored lifetime stats are incremental and never recomputed. No stat, record or badge may ever go down because of this rule.

## Manual logging
- A walk that was not live-tracked can be logged if it started within the current or previous calendar day (earlier: rejected). It creates a Walk record with no location trail, tagged as manually logged, and grants the identical reward (treats, XP, personal stats, achievements) as a finished tracked walk, with no reduced or "unverified" treatment.
- A manual log may optionally refresh the Home Range freshness of one route previously walked with real GPS tracking. Hard cap: at most 2 manual logs per local calendar day, at most 1 of them with a route refresh. A third is rejected; a second is accepted and rewarded but offers/applies no route refresh.

## Start source
- Every Walk record stores how it came to exist (live-tracked via Start Walk, or manually logged), so the two can be told apart in storage.
