# Anti-Snowball

10 layered mechanisms that keep new players competitive, welcomed, and progressing against veterans, while veterans still feel their long-term investment was worthwhile. North Star: every walk feels rewarding, every off-walk hour feels productive, every veteran feels respected, every newcomer feels welcomed.

1. **Local leaderboards by zip code:** scoped to the player's zip code, not global, so a new player competes only with immediate neighbors. A new player walking in their zip code can reach a top-3 placement within their first week.
2. **Rolling 7-day scoring:** the primary competitive metric is the trailing 7 days ("walks this week"), not lifetime XP. A daily walker who joined yesterday outscores a veteran who has not played in 6 months.
3. **Soft caps with diminishing returns:** Den building bonuses cap at +50% above level 50. On equivalent walks a level-100 player's total advantage over a level-1 player from building/level sources is approximately 30%, not more; the walk dominates outcomes, buildings tilt but do not decide.
4. **Beginner's Luck:** 2x XP and 2x treats on all qualifying actions for the first 30 days after account creation.
5. **Mentor bonuses:** a high-level player who mentors a new player gets a +5% treats bonus attributable to each walk the mentored player completes (veterans onboard newcomers rather than outcompete them).
6. **Seasonal resets (cosmetic-only prestige):** leaderboards reset every 3 months, fair for every player. Long-term players keep all upgrades; top players of the ending season get a permanent cosmetic prestige badge (e.g. "Spring 2026 Champion").
7. **Co-op over compete:** most limited-time events are collaborative, so veterans and newcomers share a team; solo competition stays the minority. Example: "walk 1000km collectively to unlock a community Spring Garden"; progress is shared across all participants regardless of level.
8. **Walk-frequency-based territory defense:** defense strength scales with how recently the owner walked, not lifetime stats, so lapsed veterans gradually lose defended territory to actively walking newcomers. A new player walking daily can defend or contest territory against a level-100 veteran who has not walked in 5 days.
9. **Asymmetric value props:** veterans get rare cosmetics, prestige badges, a large Den to display, and a deep Pack collection; newcomers get the Beginner's Luck 2x bonus, fresh territory in their own neighborhood, and beginner perks. Both sets are genuinely meaningful; neither cohort's reward set is empty.
10. **Hard power gap soft-cap:** the combined advantage from all power sources (Den building bonuses, Pack, badges) that a level-100 player can hold over a level-1 player on equivalent walks is capped at a maximum of +30% (high enough that veterans' grind is not meaningless, low enough that newcomers don't give up).
    - **Buddy's Level (Den Level) does not count:** it drives a per-walk income rate (treats earned), not a capped bonus percentage on a defense/XP/find-rate quantity; contribution is 0% however high it climbs.
    - **Fresh Ground `bloomMultiplier` does not count:** it depends only on walk geography and elapsed time (no level, building, or treat-balance term), applies identically to level-1 and level-100 players on an equivalent walk with identical cell freshness, and cancels out of the ratio the cap is defined over (contribution 0%, regardless of Level, building levels, or treat balance).
    - No future capped percentage bonus may be a function of Buddy's Level or a player's treat balance; only of building level, Pack, or badges.
