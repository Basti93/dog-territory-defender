# Game Backlog

The agile backlog of game features and ideas, grouped by theme, plus the cross-cutting constraints for every slice and a short list of known stubs and debts. Replaces the former 5-phase lean-startup plan: no phase gates. Work is pulled by owner feedback and fun; nothing here is a promise or an order.

## Prioritisation

- Pull by owner feedback and fun, not a fixed sequence. No item waits for another to be "validated"; no validation audit between slices. Any item may be built immediately regardless of theme.
- Each shipped slice gets a dated bullet in `docs/decisions/LOG.md`. Mechanics and numbers need a `game-master` fun check and a same-commit update of the relevant spec (see `CLAUDE.md`, "How work is classified").

## Repetition loop

- Ideas that make same-route walks more rewarding over time: scent strength (territory deepens visually with repeat walks; the `visits` ghost-trail brightness channel already covers a first version); 7-day streak rewards; time-of-day and seasonal collectible variations (morning vs. evening, spring blooms); an evening recap notification ("Here's what happened in your territory today"); background passive accumulation that continues on rest days with diminishing returns.
- Rewards repeating a route; never punishes a missed day (see `anti-snowball`).
- Learn: do same-route walks feel better with time? Optional hint: 4 of 5 testers still engaged on day 14 of walking the same route and liking to see their territory grow stronger.

## Social layer

- Async multiplayer that feels alive without being toxic: other players' territories on a shared map; async events (e.g. "Luna sniffed your territory at 3pm"); local leaderboard scoped to zip code only; soft territory overlap that reduces rather than destroys existing territory; rolling 7-day scoring as the primary competitive metric. Needs a backend and accounts (see Known stubs and debts).
- Claim/defend/protect language only; never destroys another player's territory.
- Learn: do other dogs visiting and friendly competition feel good? Optional hint: 20 testers in one city for 14 days, about 60% engagement, positive social feelings, no toxicity reported.

## Long-term hooks

- The long-term metagame: full Den (10+ buildable upgrades, levels 1-100, soft caps at level 50 for diminishing returns); Pack collection (recruit AI dog companions, common through rare to legendary); seasonal events (spring blooms, summer heat, autumn leaves, winter cozy); anti-snowball mechanisms (Beginner's Luck at 2x for 30 days, mentor bonuses, co-op neighborhood goals, seasonal leaderboard resets); cosmetic prestige (badges, dog accessories).
- Numeric details live in `den-economy`, `pack-collection`, `anti-snowball` and are load-bearing there: re-read the relevant spec first when pulling a Den, Pack, or anti-snowball item, and log any numeric change with its reason.
- Learn: do long-term players stay while newcomers still feel welcome? Optional hint: closed beta of about 100 users in one city for 30 days, D7 retention above 40%, D30 above 20%, no abandonment by new joiners.

## Cross-cutting constraints

Hold for every slice without restating per feature or a separate decision:

- Battery drain targets under 5% per 30-minute walk; background location tracking is opt-in.
- The walk's start point is fuzzed by 100 meters to hide the home address; GDPR compliant by default.
- Audio-first interaction (subtle dings on points-of-interest passes, optional Apple Watch / Wear OS haptics) so the phone can stay in the owner's pocket.

## Known stubs and debts

State as of 2026-10-18; recorded so nobody mistakes them for finished work. When a stub or debt is resolved, remove or update its bullet in the same change.

- No backend, no accounts, no social layer: everything is local to one phone.
- Reminders are inexact (no `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) and re-arm only when the app is opened or a walk is saved; they stop after 7 unopened days. Android OEMs with aggressive battery saving may deliver them late or never.
- Sounds and the music box are synthetic (generated by script), except the Buddy barks (public-domain recording). Music box loudness is unknown on real speakers and in a pocket.
- Buddy appears in medium size only as art (size is a uniform scale); markings are overlays on the head layer and follow head tilt by a few px; accent colour is baked in; pivots are hard-coded in `imageSkinSources.ts`.
- Territory names come from reverse-geocoding and stay in the language they were created in.
- Den art has 3 growth stages for building levels up to 100 (thresholds picked by eye).
- Map tiles need internet (no offline tiles, the map is blank-ish without signal); the Viertel band is a live embedded map, so it is blank-ish without signal too.
- `src/app` hooks are covered by the component harness but untested on device; SQLite migrations (guarded `ALTER TABLE`s) are untested at the SQLite level; no crash reporting and no CI (`npm run check` is run by hand).
- Backlog themes above (streaks, leaderboards, Pack, and so on) are intentionally absent until pulled.
