# Personal stats and achievements (2026-08-16, shipped)
**Why:** Phase 1 had no "highscore to chase" across walks (only Den building levels). Leaderboards (needs other players) and streaks (needs day tracking) were out of reach; a purely personal milestone system needs neither. Came out of a 3-pass process: two design drafts plus a game-master compliance review.
**Decisions:**
- Standalone `personal-stats` capability, not folded into `den-economy` (spans walk lifecycle and Den economy, needs its own scoping and Trophy Hall handoff).
- Lifetime stats: walks closed, distance, lifetime territory area, biggest territory, longest walk, lifetime treats earned, current Den levels. Derived incrementally from existing `Walk`/`TerritoryPolygon`/`DenEconomyState`; no new fields on `src/types.ts` (no `endTime` on `Walk`). Lifetime territory area is a flavor sum of closed polygons, not deduplicated against re-walked ground.
- Zero mechanical bonus from any achievement (prestige only). Trophy Hall (`+0.5%` per badge, `den-economy`) is the only system that may attach a bonus; the spec references that figure instead of restating it so they cannot drift.
- 18 threshold achievements, no streak or other-player triggers. Walks closed: 1, 10, 25, 50, 100 (First Steps, Paws on the Pavement, Familiar Trails, Neighborhood Legend, 100 Walks One Very Good Dog). Ground Claimed: first walk with territory >=50 m2. Lifetime distance: 5 / 25 / 100 km (Round the Block, Cross-Town Trotter, Century Trail). Long Way Round: single walk >=3,000 m. Biggest territory: >=1,000 m2 (Big Backyard) / >=5,000 m2 (Whole City Block). Lifetime treats: 100 / 1,000 / 10,000 (First Bakery Batch, Treat Jar Overflowing, Treats for Days). Moving In: first building upgrade. Handy Homebody: any building level 10. Cozy Den: Doghouse + Bakery + Sniff Garden all >= level 5. Copy is warm/spring/dog-themed.
- Lifetime treats must feed from both earning paths: `awardWalkTreats` and `applyPassiveAccrual` (load-time effect), via the same `recordTreatsEarned(stats, amount)`. A walk-close-only hook would undercount (caught in review).
- UI: walk-triggered unlocks extend the celebration payload with optional `unlockedAchievements` (small banner under the tease box); Den-triggered unlocks reuse the Den overflow-notice banner; one new screen, `StatsScreen.tsx`, opened from a header button.
- Storage: new tables `personal_stats` (singleton row) and `achievements`, created with `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`; repository trio `PersonalStatsRepository` / sqlite / in-memory.
- No backfill from pre-existing walks (4 old walks showed 0 on device); declined as unneeded complexity for the single-tester stage. Revisit before wider rollout (replay closed walks chronologically through `recordWalkClosed`).
**Rejected:**
- Parallel "Player/Den Level and XP" system, parked: reopens the earlier deferral (post-walk celebration, 2026-08-16) of dog XP/level display, leaked Phase 4 unlock ladder into Phase 1 UI, baked in Beginner's Luck (Phase 4 only), and its Phase-1 payoff was redundant with walk-count achievements. Needs an explicit owner decision to reopen.
- Deduplicated unique-coverage territory stat, because that is a much harder computation nobody needs.
**Superseded:** the Celebration and Den screens were removed 2026-10-18 (postcards and Album replaced them), so the unlock surfaces above changed; `StatsScreen.tsx` and the repository remain.
