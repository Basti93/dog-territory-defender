- 2026-10-18 · Game design (walk lifecycle, `docs/specs/walk-lifecycle.md`): a tracked walk that went nowhere is not saved. New rule `walkHasMovement` (`src/utils/geometry.ts`): no movement = walked distance under 50 m (`NO_MOVEMENT_MAX_DISTANCE_M`) AND under 13 painted cells (`NO_MOVEMENT_CELLS_BELOW`, both in `src/utils/grid.ts`). Old: every finished tracked walk was saved and uploaded, even one started by mistake. New: such a walk is discarded (no record, route, cells, stats, `walk.upload` or homecoming) on Finish (closed like a cancel, Buddy: "Noch keine Strecke, nichts zu speichern."), on auto-finish and on cold-start restore (both quiet); manual logs are untouched. The owner's brief said "painted cells < 2"; measured, a walk that stays on one spot already paints about 5 cells (25 m paint radius, 20 m cells) and 50 m of walking about 12, so 2 could never hold; 13 keeps the cell half meaningful as a backstop. Jitter rule (owner decision): a walk is also "no movement" when every fix lies within `NO_MOVEMENT_RADIUS_M` = 40 m of the first fix (max haversine distance from the start), even when GPS noise summed up to more than 50 m; so the full rule is "within 40 m of the start, OR (under 50 m walked AND under 13 cells)". Cancel is now easy and calm: a "Runde abbrechen" link under Finish on the live map, a small "abbrechen" link under the scene's gate sign while a walk runs, one question in a PaperNote ("Runde verwerfen? Diese Runde wird nicht gespeichert." / "Verwerfen" / "Weiterlaufen") only when the walk has moved, then back to the scene with Buddy's "Dann eben später!". Bug found on the way: tapping the "Gassi gehen" sign (or the gate, or Buddy) during a running walk started a SECOND walk and replaced the first one's trail; during a walk the sign now reads "Zur Runde" and opens the live map, and Buddy no longer starts walks at all (tapping him only barks; the gate and its sign are the only starters).
- 2026-10-18 · Architecture/Native rebuild (runtime 0.2.0): `expo-av` replaced by `expo-audio` (~1.1.1), one native rebuild bundles it with the config fixes below. Why: expo-av is deprecated in SDK 54 and its players caused the dev-reload crash "Player is accessed on the wrong thread". Behaviour kept: `src/feedback/sounds.ts` and `src/app/useAmbientMusic.ts` use the imperative `createAudioPlayer` (synchronous, one preloaded player per sound, restart = `seekTo(0)` only when the player has moved, then `play()`); a player that throws on use (released, e.g. after Fast Refresh) is removed and re-created once; Fast Refresh releases the previous players (`player.remove()`); `stopAllSounds` now pauses every player; no sound in the background (`shouldPlayInBackground: false`, `play()` still guards on AppState; the music-box status listener will not restart music unless AppState is active); silent switch still wins on iOS (`playsInSilentMode: false`); interruption mode is `mixWithOthers` on iOS and `doNotMix` on Android, i.e. exactly what expo-av resolved to before (expo-audio has one mode for both platforms, so it is chosen per platform). The `expo-audio` plugin is configured with `microphonePermission: false, recordAudioAndroid: false` plus `android.blockedPermissions` RECORD_AUDIO (expo-audio's library manifest declares it itself; we never record; the default would add a mic prompt string and RECORD_AUDIO). `devWarnings.ts` (hid the expo-av deprecation banner) removed. Config: `expo.version` 0.1.0 -> 0.2.0 (also `package.json`); `runtimeVersion` policy is `appVersion`, so builds on 0.1.0 no longer get updates published for 0.2.0, intended since the native layer changed. Found while checking the location plugin: the key `locationAlwaysAndWhenInUsePermissions` (plural) was a typo and ignored, so iOS showed Expo's default text; now `locationAlwaysAndWhenInUsePermission`, `locationAlwaysPermission` and `locationWhenInUsePermission` carry friendly English purpose strings (no localized InfoPlist exists). `UIBackgroundModes` has `location` (no `audio`, since nothing sounds in the background).
- 2026-10-18 · Game design (game-master approved): walked distance now ignores implausible steps. Old behaviour: `cumulativeDistance` summed every step between fixes, so GPS jumps, tram or car rides during a running walk and the gap after an iOS force-quit inflated distance, XP and kilometres (`paintedCells` already skipped steps over 60 m or 4.5 m/s, distance did not). New rule (`stepCountsAsDistance`, constants beside the paint filter in `src/utils/grid.ts`): a step counts if (normal) at most 60 m AND at most 4.5 m/s, or (dropout bridge) 60-250 m AND at most 180 s AND average at most 2.0 m/s; missing or zero time difference counts only up to 60 m. Why: stop rewarding rides and jumps without punishing an honest tunnel or sleeping phone. Silent: no "x m not counted" anywhere. Not retroactive: `cumulativeDistance` is now the filtered sum, `cumulativeDistanceLegacy` the old one, and `walkDistanceMeters(walk)` picks by `startTime` against `DISTANCE_RULE_SINCE_MS` (2026-10-18 19:35 UTC), so walks started earlier keep their old distance wherever it is recomputed from points (Walk Portrait, prior-walk XP average in `walkRewards.ts`, partner-walk note in `sync/apply.ts`, server `services/walks.ts` XP and `neighbours/coop.ts` weekly km); lifetime stats and records are stored incrementally and never recomputed. App and server share the code via `@game/utils/geometry`. Live map distance (`MapScreen`) and finish distance (`finishTrackedWalk`) use the same function. Trail lines are cut at failing steps (`splitAtGaps`, web copy in `src/web/gapSplitScript.ts` with an agreement test), used by the live Trampelpfad, Alex's thread and the Walk Portrait. Known edge: the server simplifies a partner's presence thread to 400 points, so on a very long straight stretch a simplified step above 250 m or 180 s can show a gap in the thread (presentation only).

- 2026-10-18 · Bug fix/Architecture: a walk in flight survives closing the app. Cause: the active walk lived only in memory (`useWalkSession`'s `walkRef`), so after a swipe-away or kill, a tap on the "Tails Up" notification or the icon opened a fresh Doorstep and the walk was gone. Now `ActiveWalkRepository` (SQLite tables `active_walk` + `active_walk_fixes`, created IF NOT EXISTS; in-memory fake for tests) is the durable mirror; `walkRef` stays the source of truth while running. `activeWalkRecorder.ts` writes it in batches (every 10 fixes or 15 s, and on AppState background) from `gps.ts`'s `deliver()` via `setFixRecorder`, so fixes reaching a UI-less or headless-relaunched runtime are saved too; started in `startWalk`, cleared after the finished walk is saved and on cancel. At launch (`useWalkRestore`, once the first-launch state is loaded): newest fix under 3 h old and auto-finish (replayed over the stored fixes, `restoreWalk.ts`) not done -> `resumeWalk` (same points/start/mode, location task restarted, live map opens, `WALK_RESTORED`); older than 3 h, or auto-finish says done -> normal `finishTrackedWalk` path (`WALK_RESTORE_FINISHED`, endedBy `auto-still`/`auto-home`, celebration as usual); fewer than 2 fixes and older than 3 h -> discarded quietly. A walk id that is already saved is dropped, a busy session is never touched (no duplicate walk). With no stored walk, a location task that outlived its walk is stopped. The Android foreground-service notification is expo-location's own (plain launcher intent, no payload), so a tap is not distinguishable from an icon tap: routing to the map happens on restore only; a warm resume never navigates. iOS: after a force quit updates stop until the app opens again; the walk resumes with a gap. `paintedCells` already refuses any step over 60 m or 4.5 m/s, so a jump paints nothing; distance (`cumulativeDistance`) is deliberately unchanged, so a long jump still counts as distance (open point, see report). No game numbers changed; the 3 h and 15 s/10 fix values are plain constants in `restoreWalk.ts` / `activeWalkRecorder.ts`.
