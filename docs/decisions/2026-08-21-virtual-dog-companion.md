# Virtual dog companion "Buddy Levels Up" (2026-08-21, shipped)
**Why:** the Den Level/XP number climbed on every walk but gated and granted nothing (only unlocks buildings 4-10, which did not exist). Fix: the Level is the player's own dog's level and visibly makes each walk pay more (cookie-clicker: a number that makes other numbers grow). Numbers-only, no new art (no artist; Phase 3 plans real dog photos, which cannot be "redrawn" per stage).
**Decisions:**
- Guardrails: bonuses only rise; no guilt/sad/neglected imagery; one dog (never a second named character); no final stage ("/100", percentage); no fake agency (levels only from real walks); level-up beat lives inside the walk-close celebration. Titles ship only alongside the payout.
- Displayed number = full treat payout. `awardWalkTreats` was flat 50/walk (no distance/area term), so "Buddy brings home N treats a walk" is exact. Clocks: walk quality -> XP -> Level; Level -> treats.
- `buddyPayout(L) = round(50 * 1.055^(L-1))` (`BALANCE.leveling.buddyPayoutGrowth`, `src/economy/formulas.ts`). L1 50, L2 53, L10 81, L20 138, L30 236, L50 689, L100 10 022. Step is >= +3 at every level-up (raw step >= 2.75), constant 5.5% relative.
- Level takes 0 pp of anti-snowball's +30% power budget because treats are income, not power: the only sink is hard-capped, exponentially priced buildings, so income only speeds reaching the same ceiling. Proof: a 30% cap over 99 steps allows only 0.265%/level uniform; 1%/level hits 2.7x at L100. Invariant: no bonus percentage may ever be a function of Buddy's Level or treat balance. This consciously reopens `den-level-xp`'s "zero percentage gameplay impact" Non-Goal (it protected the cap, which still holds).
- Power ledger (sums to +30%): Level 0, Sniff Garden 8, Training Yard 6, Watch Tower 6, Spring Garden 3, Trophy Hall 3, Pack 4 (Phase 4 ones reserved). Realised L100 vs L1: +29.59%.
- `sniffGardenPct(bl) = 8 * (1 - e^(-bl/25)) / (1 - e^(-4))` applied to treats (bl1 0.32%, bl10 2.69%, bl25 5.15%, bl50 7.05%, bl100 8.00%). Effect changed from "+X% collectibles" to "+X% treats found on a walk" (no collectible currency existed). No reverse dependency: level is a pure function of XP.
- `storageCap(dh, L) = max(buddyPayout(L) * 4 * 1.5^(dh-1), 1.25 * cheapest available upgrade)`: equals old `100+100*dh` at dh 1-2; 1.5 = Doghouse costMult; the 1.25x floor is a never-softlock guarantee. The old formula stalled the Den (dh10: cap 1 100 vs cheapest upgrade 2 306); 3-year sim 8/6/8 buildings vs 24/20/25 with the fix. `passiveTreatsPerHour(bl, L) = bl * 1 * buddyPayout(L)/50`. Building costs deliberately do not scale.
- Milestone titles at levels 2, 4, 7, 11, 16, 22, 29, 37, then every 8 (`gap(k) = min(k, 8)`); no final title. Authored copy only for first 8 (45+ keeps the 8th title). Walks to reach: 2, 5, 11, 23, 49, 113, 277, 750 (@1.13 curve).
- Cliffhanger fires at walk-close when `xpToNextLevel <= trailing 5-walk avg XP` (fallback 82.5) and next level is a milestone; once per level, never a notification (~1 per 250 walks).
- Drop `treatsPerLevelUp: 25`: tester's eye goes to the Level number; one event, one reward ("Buddy reached Level 8 - he brings home 53 treats a walk now, up from 50").
- `xpGrowth` 1.13 -> 1.10 now (`balanceConfig.ts` is 1.1): walks to L30 314 -> 181, L50 3 710 -> 1 282, gap at L40 142 -> 50 walks; first two weeks nearly identical (L10 at 17 vs 19 walks). Existing saves get a free jump that must show as an explicit moment ("Buddy caught up"), see `denEconomy.ts`.
- Keepsakes (designed only, no code found; flat p = 0.18 per qualifying walk, hard pity 12 walks, 2-walk cooldown; mean gap 6.7) are mechanically inert, outside every cap, rate never touched by Level/buildings.
- Exploit mitigations (walk-reward exploit fix, now `DAILY_REWARD_MULTIPLIERS = [1,1,1,0.5,0.25,0.1]` in `src/economy/walkRewards.ts`): per-day allowance applied to treats and the flat XP base 20 only; qualifying walk >= 200 m and >= 5 min; manual logs share the allowance and are capped at 2/day. Found: 20 x 100 m loops = 10x treats, manual log taps = 150x.
- Den copy reframe ("Sniff Garden means Buddy comes home with 124 instead of 118") is narrative only.
**Rejected:**
- Level-only bonus term displayed separately, because the split did not exist and the number would be smaller.
- Souvenir/keepsake/yard accumulation layers ("The Yard Grows With You") and visible dog growth, because art-dependent; deferred. Hunger/happiness meters, sad states, elder stage, streak framing, bars/hearts over the dog, seasonal limited cosmetics, because they turn absence into damage or guilt.
- xpGrowth 1.08, because L50 inside year one burns the horizon.
**Risks:** late Den upgrade cadence stretches (976 walks per upgrade by year 3); huge storage makes overflow notifications rare; walk-count figures assume 1.5 km / 8 000 m^2 twice a day (82.5 XP).
