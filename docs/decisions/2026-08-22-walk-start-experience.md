# Walk-start experience (2026-08-22, concept; MVP shipped, widget and reminder later/deferred)
**Why:** A dog owner walks up to ~4 times a day, mostly short routine "out the door" walks. In that leash-in-hand moment the player does not open the app and tap Start Walk, and an untracked walk is gone (no territory, treats, reward), which erodes the habit. The fix is not a louder nudge but removing the decision at the door and making a miss harmless.
**Decisions:**
- Concept, two parts: (1) "The Dog at the Door", a home/lock-screen widget dog, one tap starts tracking (dozing, perking up near usual walk times; never sad); (2) "Nothing Was Lost", claiming an untracked walk retroactively with identical rewards.
- Guiding principles: zero decisions at the door; the dog is never sad (no decay, no guilt copy or imagery); a miss is a gift, not a loss; no artificial urgency (no countdown, no expiring window); numbers only ever go up; a retro-claimed walk is never lesser (no "unverified", no reduced treats, no different visual treatment).
- Notification rule (resolved by two game-master passes): a notification may fire only if something already true is sitting unclaimed (overflowed treats, finished Den build, claimable walk). The clock (learned coarse time-of-day bucket) decides only WHEN, never WHY. No qualifying condition means silence.
- A genuinely clock-fired "time to walk" reminder is only conditionally fun-safe, as a time-boxed experiment: opt-in, player sets their own time, one per day, silent, auto-sleep after 3 consecutive ignores with no "we noticed" message, never fires if that walk is already tracked/claimed, one-tap permanent dismissal, 4-week flag with a pre-agreed kill condition. Test the friction hypothesis (start moment) first; the reminder may not be needed.
- Accepted copy: "Looks like you two were out this morning — a walk is waiting to be claimed." / "Your Treat Bakery overflowed! 124 treats waiting." (124 illustrative). Rejected copy: "Time for your afternoon walk!" (order on the app's clock), "Buddy's waiting by the door" (guilt mascot at a real animal), "It's been 2 days since your last walk" (number rises when you do less), "Claim today's territory before midnight!" (invented urgency). Test: each must pass the "worst day of the month" check.
- MVP scope (decided): earned-trigger notification, in-app "Log a walk we missed" button (manual, no automatic inference), `Walk.startSource` (`button` | `manual-log`) tag. Delivered by `docs/decisions/2026-08-21-walk-start-notifications.md`. The scheduled reminder was later built as `docs/decisions/walk-reminder.md`. The widget and automatic missed-walk detection remain unbuilt and gated on MVP data (no new proposal exists yet).
- Widget feasibility: a real Android App Widget needs leaving the dev-client workflow for `expo prebuild`/EAS plus a config plugin with native Kotlin; iOS WidgetKit has no clean managed-Expo path. Automatic "was I walking" detection needs new always-on sensing (current foreground-service tracking in `src/utils/gps.ts` only runs during a walk) with battery/GDPR cost.
- Open at the time: whether a log needs a look-back cap (answered: today or yesterday, see the notifications decision).
**Rejected (anti-patterns, none to be shipped):**
- Guilt mascot / "you made him sad" (Duolingo), because it weaponises the bond with a real animal; never ship a sad-dog state.
- Breakable streak counters (Snapchat), because one missed day (illness, travel) wipes a big number; any future streak is a rolling bonus, never a visible number snapping to zero.
- Time-boxed "late" submissions (BeReal) and countdown lures / limited-time spawns (Pokémon GO), because they put the walk on the app's schedule, not the dog's.
- Pace/distance social comparison (Strava), because a routine 5-minute pee-break walk must not read as a failure; keep comparison away from start/claim.
- Loss-framed copy ("3 walks missed", "treats expiring"); always a gain sitting there, never something slipping away.
